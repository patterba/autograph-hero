A Hall of Fame autograph usually runs $100 or more. This month you can get seven of them for $60 or less, and they're all under one roof.

We looked at every November signing listed on Autograph Hero and compared only the autograph tickets: no photo ops, no inscriptions, no authentication add-ons. The cheapest Hall of Famers left this month are all at the Chicago Sports Spectacular, Nov 20–22, at the Donald E. Stephens Convention Center in Rosemont, IL.

The list

1. Michael Cooper (Basketball HOF, 2024): $49

Saturday, Nov 21. $49 for a flat up to 16x20, $59 for any other item. The Showtime Lakers' defensive stopper is a bargain here: the same autograph is $69–$99 through a mail-in signing.

2. Jari Kurri (Hockey HOF, 2001): $50

Sunday, Nov 22. $50 for a flat or puck, $60 for any other item, and the "HOF '01" inscription is included free. Gretzky's longtime Oilers linemate costs $69–$89 elsewhere this month.

3. Doug Gilmour (Hockey HOF, 2011): $50

Sunday, Nov 22. $50 for a flat or puck, $60 for any other item, and the "HOF '11" inscription is free. Compare that with $69–$89 through mail-in.

4. Fergie Jenkins (Baseball HOF, 1991): $59

Sunday, Nov 22. $59 for a flat or ball, $79 for anything else. The mail-in price for the Cubs legend starts at $99.

5. Orlando Pace (Football HOF, 2016): $60

Saturday, Nov 21. $60 covers any item, including a full-size helmet. The mail-in price starts at $99.

6. Andre Dawson (Baseball HOF, 2010): $60

Sunday, Nov 22. $60 for a flat or ball, $80 for any other item. "The Hawk" is $89 and up through mail-in.

7. Bobby Bell (Football HOF, 1983): $60

Friday, Nov 20. $60 for a flat or mini helmet, $75 for anything else, with the "HOF '83" inscription free. Can't make it to Chicago? WSC Sports in Waukesha, WI has him the same day at the same price.

Missed it already?

Earlier this month brought even lower prices: Marcel Dionne for $19 (cards), Lindsay Whalen for $20 and Bob Lilly for $35. Sign up for Autograph Hero alerts so you hear about the next deals before they're gone.

Tips before you buy

  • Check the item tier. The lowest price usually covers flats (photos and cards) or balls. Jerseys and helmets cost more.
  • Pre-order online. Tickets for popular signers can sell out before the show.
  • Free inscriptions add value. Kurri, Gilmour and Bell all include their HOF year at no extra cost.
