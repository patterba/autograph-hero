Get the Autograph Hero app

Put every signing one tap from your home screen. No app store and nothing to download: it installs from your browser in a few seconds and opens full screen, like any other app.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open autographhero.com in Safari.
  2. Tap Share. Don't see it? Tap ••• or ☰ to open the menu, then tap Share.
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open autographhero.com in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install when it's offered, or the ⋮ menu, then Add to home screen.
  3. Find Autograph Hero with your other apps.

Pages you've opened keep working with no signal, which helps in a crowded convention hall. Listings still refresh every hour when you're online.

About Autograph Hero

Autograph Hero is a passion project built by a lifelong autograph and sports card collector. I made it so fans can find every signing, meet & greet and mail-in in one place, and never miss the chance to get something signed by the people they root for. Read the story · Contact us

Listings are gathered from promoter and shop websites and refreshed every week. Dates, prices and availability can change, so always confirm with the organizer before you order or travel.
