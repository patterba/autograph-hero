Privacy policy

Last updated Friday, October 30, 2026

Autograph Hero lists upcoming autograph signings and meet & greets. We collect as little as we can, we never sell your information, and you can delete your account yourself at any time.

What we collect and why

  • When you sign up: your name, email address, a password, the teams, players and people you pick, and when you agreed to receive alerts. We use these to email you when your favorites have a signing and to let you log in and manage your account. Your password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form by our login provider; nobody at Autograph Hero can see it.
  • When you log in: your login is remembered in your browser's storage on that device for up to 3 days, or until you log out, along with a copy of your favorites so the home page can show your signings. If you forget your password, we email you a link to set a new one.
  • Reminders: if you ask for a reminder on a signing page, we keep your email and which signing it's for, send one reminder email (plus a confirmation with a cancel link), and delete the record 30 days after it's sent. Deleting your account removes your reminders too.
  • The app and offline pages: your browser keeps copies of the pages you open so they work without a signal, and remembers if you closed the "Get the app" banner. These stay on your device and are never sent to us. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes them.
  • When you submit a signing: the event details you enter, plus your name (optional) and email address so we can follow up. We check every submission before it goes on the calendar. Your name and email are never shown on the site.
  • When you contact us: your name (optional), email address, topic and message. We email it to ourselves so we can reply, and keep a copy to keep track of conversations. We don't add you to any mailing list.
  • To stop spam: a scrambled, one-way code made from your internet (IP) address, used to limit how many forms one visitor can send in an hour. The Submit a Signing form also uses a Cloudflare Turnstile check to tell people from bots.
  • Weekly alerts: once a week, if new signings were added for your favorites, we email you a short list with photos and links. Weeks with nothing new, you get nothing.
  • Site statistics: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and popular pages. It does not use cookies and does not follow you to other websites.
  • Unique visitor count: to count how many different people visit each day, each page sends us your IP address and browser name, which we immediately scramble together with a random code that changes every day. We keep only the scrambled result, which can't be turned back into your IP address or linked from one day to the next. No cookies are used.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent your information, we don't show ads, and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies. We only email you about signings for the favorites you chose, and about your account (such as login links).

Who helps us run the site

These services handle some information on our behalf, only to run Autograph Hero:

  • Supabase: stores sign-ups and submissions, and handles logins.
  • Resend: sends the welcome email, login links and alert emails.
  • Cloudflare: site statistics and the spam check.
  • Bluehost: hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts, Wikimedia Commons and promoter websites: the site's fonts and some photos load from these services, so they receive your IP address when your browser fetches them, as with any website.

Your choices

  • Change your favorites or turn alerts off on your account page.
  • Delete your account there too. That removes your name, email and favorites.
  • Every alert email includes a link to stop alerts.
  • To ask what we hold about you, or anything else, use our contact form, or the account page to manage or delete your details.

How long we keep it

We keep your sign-up until you delete your account or ask us to. Submitted signings are kept as a record of the listings on the site. The scrambled spam-check codes are only used to count recent sends.

Children

Autograph Hero isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has signed up, use our contact form, or the account page to manage or delete your details and we'll remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change matters for how we use your email, we'll tell members by email before it takes effect.

About Autograph Hero

Autograph Hero is a passion project built by a lifelong autograph and sports card collector. I made it so fans can find every signing, meet & greet and mail-in in one place, and never miss the chance to get something signed by the people they root for. Read the story · Contact us
