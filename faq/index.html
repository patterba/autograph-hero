Getting started

What is Autograph Hero?

Autograph Hero is one place to find upcoming autograph signings, meet & greets and mail-in signings. We gather them from promoter and shop websites so you don't have to check dozens of pages. Athletes come first, but you'll also find actors, musicians, authors and artists. See every upcoming signing.

Is it free?

Yes. Browsing signings, signing up for alerts and setting reminders are all free.

Do I need an account?

No. You can browse every signing without one. Sign up (free) to pick your favorite teams, players and people. You'll then get email alerts and a personal feed of their signings.

Is there an app?

Yes, and it's free. It installs straight from your browser on iPhone, iPad or Android, no app store needed. Here's how to add it to your home screen.

Finding signings

Where do your listings come from?

We check promoter, card shop, convention and bookstore websites every week, and fans send in the ones we miss. Every fan submission is checked before it goes live. The site refreshes every hour, and every listing links back to the promoter or shop running the signing.

What's the difference between in-person, private and mail-in signings?
  • In person: you go to the event (a show, card shop or convention), meet the signer and get your item signed in front of you.
  • Private signing: you send or drop off your item, or buy one from the promoter, before the order deadline. It's signed without you there and shipped back.
  • Mail-in: you order from home and everything is handled by mail. These ship anywhere, so they don't depend on where you live.

Some signings offer more than one of these.

What kinds of signers do you list?

Browse by category: Sports, Entertainment, Musicians, Authors and Artists. You can also look up a signer A–Z or a team.

How do I find signings near me?

Pick your state in the "Where" box on the home page, or open Signings by State. Choose "Mail-in · ships anywhere" to see signings you can order from wherever you are.

A signing I know about isn't listed. Can I add it?

Please do. Use Submit A Signing and fill in what you know, or just upload a photo of the flyer and we'll read the details from it. We check every submission before it goes live.

What does "Rumor" mean on a listing?

A rumor is a signing we've heard about but the promoter hasn't confirmed yet. Treat it as a heads-up, and wait for the official announcement before you make plans.

What if a signing is canceled or changes?

We update listings when the promoter does, but plans can change quickly. Always check the details with the promoter before you order or travel. If you spot something wrong, let us know.

Can I see signings that already happened?

Yes. The Signing Archives list past signings by month, newest first.

Alerts and reminders

How do alerts work?

Sign up and pick your favorite teams, players and people. Every Friday we email you any new signings for them, plus a few more coming up soon. If nothing new was added for your favorites that week, we don't email you.

Can I get a reminder for one signing?

Yes. On any upcoming signing's page, tap "Email me a reminder." For in-person signings we email you about a day before. For private and mail-in signings we email you the day before the order deadline. You can also add a signing to your calendar from the same page.

How do I change my favorites or stop alerts?

Log in and open "Change Preferences" to add or remove favorites or pause alerts. Every alert email also has a link at the bottom to stop alerts.

Buying and authenticity

Do you sell tickets or autographs?

No. Autograph Hero is a free listing site, not a store. Every listing links to the promoter or shop running the signing, and they handle tickets, orders and shipping.

I have a problem with my order. Who do I contact?

The promoter or shop you ordered from. We don't take orders, so we can't change, track or refund them.

Are the autographs authentic?

Authentication is up to each promoter. Many offer certificates or holograms from known authenticators such as JSA, PSA or Beckett, often as an add-on. If you're not sure how a signing is authenticated, ask the promoter before you order.

What does the "From" price mean?

It's the lowest autograph price the promoter lists. Add-ons like authentication and inscriptions cost extra and aren't included. The signing page shows the full price list.

For promoters and shops

How do I get my signings listed?

Listing is free. Use Submit A Signing for a single event. If you run signings regularly, send us a message with your website and we'll add it to the sites we check every week.

Can I advertise on Autograph Hero?

Yes. Get in touch and tell us what you'd like to promote.

One of our listings has a mistake. How do I fix it?

Send us a message with the signing's link and what needs to change, and we'll correct it.
